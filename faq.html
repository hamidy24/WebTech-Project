Frequently asked questions

Answers for the Phase 1 website demonstration.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. Browse and add products to the cart without signing in. An account session is required before submitting a demo order.

How do I choose size and color?

Open a product page and choose an available size/color combination. Each combination has its own SKU and sample stock.

Does payment transfer real money?

No. bKash, Nagad, card and Cash on Delivery are demonstration choices. No online payment is processed.

How does the coupon work?

Enter an active coupon at checkout and choose Apply. The prototype checks expiry and minimum order before calculating the discount.

Will cart and stock be shared across devices?

No. Phase 1 uses browser-local storage. A backend/database is needed to synchronize accounts, inventory and orders.

How do I print the receipt?

After demo checkout, use Print receipt / Save PDF on the receipt page.

How do I contact the store?

Use Contact Us. The form is a local demo and does not send messages.
