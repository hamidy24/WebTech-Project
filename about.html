Our story

Men's essentials, made simple.

BRUTBOX & CO. is a student-built e-commerce concept for everyday underwear and considered accessories.

What we focus on

The concept brings underwear, bow ties, silk pocket squares, socks, eyeglass cases and wallets into one place. Shoppers can review product details, selected size/color availability, cart totals and order information before placing a demonstration order.

Our design approach

The website uses navy, warm neutrals and gold, consistent product cards, Playfair Display headings and Inter interface text. No large promotional banners are used; products and categories appear directly.

About this project

This is Phase 1 of an academic Web Technologies project. It demonstrates frontend interactions with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and browser storage. It is not a production shop and does not process real payments.
