Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

Plainspoken, friendly terms designed to make sizing and gifting worry-free across Bangladesh.

14-Day Unworn Guarantee

If a bow tie, pocket square, wallet, or eyeglass case is not quite right, you can return or exchange it within 14 calendar days of delivery.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and complete with tags and gift box.
  • Keep your digital or 80 mm printed order number ready (e.g. BB-1042).

Hygiene Seal for Underwear & Socks

For customer hygiene, men's underwear and socks may only be returned or exchanged when their factory hygiene seal and packaging remain unopened and intact.

  • Opened underwear packs are eligible only if there is a verified stitching or fabric defect upon arrival.
  • Need help choosing a waist size? Check the Size Guide on every product page first.

Fast Refunds & Exchanges

Once your return parcel arrives at our Banani atelier, inspection takes one business day.

  • bKash / Nagad / Card orders: refunded to the same wallet or card within 3–5 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: refunded via bKash/Nagad transfer or instant store credit.
  • Size exchanges: dispatched the very next morning at zero extra courier fee for Dhaka Metro.

How to Start a Return

No complicated forms required—reach out with your order number and we arrange doorstep pickup.

  • Message us via our Contact page or call +880 1711-000111 (Sat–Thu, 10 AM – 8 PM).
  • You can also drop off unworn exchanges in person at House 14, Road 11, Banani, Dhaka 1213.
