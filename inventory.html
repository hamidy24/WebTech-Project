Stock rules

  • Each SKU represents one sellable size/color combination.
  • Stock cannot be reduced below zero by a cart.
  • Demo order confirmation decreases the purchased SKU.
  • Restocking increases only the chosen SKU.
  • Low stock means five or fewer units in this prototype.
A real multi-user shop needs server-side validation and database transactions to prevent overselling.
