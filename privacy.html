Privacy information

Privacy Policy

Prototype privacy explanation. Production wording must match the real data practices.

Data in this demo

Demo profiles, cart, wishlist, sample orders, inventory and admin activity may be stored in browser localStorage. Customer login state and per-tab admin session tokens use sessionStorage.

Do not enter sensitive information

Do not enter real passwords, card numbers, payment PINs, identity information or private customer details. Authentication and payment screens are simulations, not secure services.

Sharing and storage

Browser-local data is not automatically available to other visitors, devices or admins. The demo does not send orders or contact-form data to a central server.

Future production system

A real service must define lawful purposes, access controls, retention, security measures, contact details and third-party processors before launch.
